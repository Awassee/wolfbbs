# WolfBBS Design Context

## Target audience
- Retro BBS enthusiasts who want a nostalgic, ANSI-first community system.
- Sysops who need modern setup, moderation, and diagnostics without losing the classic feel.
- New users who may discover WolfBBS first through the web UI before trying SSH/IRC.

## Core use cases
- First-run setup and launch-readiness checks for a new board.
- Daily community use: boards, chat, mail, doors, status, and lightweight discovery.
- Sysop operation: moderation, configuration, user management, files, events, and health checks.
- Cross-surface continuity between SSH/TUI, web admin, and chat tools.

## Brand personality and tone
- Nostalgic terminal soul with modern operational polish.
- Confident, legible, slightly dramatic, but never gimmicky.
- Friendly to hobbyists, serious enough for operators.
- Feels like a lovingly restored classic system, not generic SaaS and not AI-generated chrome.

## Product constraints
- The text interface is the primary product surface; web should support and extend it, not overpower it.
- Preserve classic BBS cues: clear hierarchy, strong status visibility, memorable headers, operator trust signals, caller identity, and community warmth.
- Avoid crowded chrome, redundant controls, and dashboard noise.
- Web UI should stay responsive on mobile and desktop, with accessible contrast and predictable interaction patterns.
- Avoid generic AI aesthetics: default Inter/system stacks, purple gradients, meaningless metric cards, decorative glass for its own sake, and nested card soup.

## Preferred visual direction
- Modernized retro-futurist utility rather than consumer SaaS minimalism.
- Deep tinted neutrals, disciplined accents, and purposeful motion.
- Bold typography with clear information density control.
- Interfaces should feel organized, sharp, and tactile, with selective delight rather than constant ornament.

## UI quality bar
- Distinctive and memorable.
- Operationally clear under stress.
- Nostalgic without becoming cramped or kitschy.
- Consistent across setup, admin, and caller journeys.
