# WolfBBS v1.1.7

This release is a product-manager pass focused on reducing first-run confusion for operators and making launch readiness obvious inside the product.

## Highlights

- new `/admin/launch` operator home base with launch summary, runtime checks, next-best actions, and operator command references
- admin dashboard now surfaces a launch digest instead of forcing sysops to infer readiness from scattered pages
- `/status` and `/config` now point operators into a clear launch flow
- `/connect` now helps callers choose the right client and shows a first-call checklist
- guided guest tour now explains how to become a caller and why people come back
- boards now show a sysop-specific launch helper when the board list is empty
- new `docs/OPERATOR_PLAYBOOK.md` for launch-day rhythm, screen selection, recovery order, and anti-patterns

## Why it matters

WolfBBS now gives a new sysop a single control point for launch readiness instead of making them bounce between setup, status, config, and docs. Caller-facing entry pages are also clearer, which reduces trial-and-error during first use.
