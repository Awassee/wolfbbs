# WolfBBS v1.1.6

This release is a product usability pass focused on first-run clarity, operator confidence, and launch readiness.

## Highlights

- installer now writes `FIRST_STEPS.txt` with the exact first-login and launch flow
- `bash install.sh --status` now writes `SERVICE_STATUS.txt` and prints a launch verdict
- `bash install.sh --doctor` now groups blockers and warnings and points to concrete next commands
- new operator docs: `LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` and `TROUBLESHOOTING.md`
- `/help` now includes a 10-minute launch plan and symptom routing
- `/admin/setup` now includes dynamic launch-readiness checks and next-best actions

## Why it matters

WolfBBS now behaves more like a product and less like a codebase. A new operator can install it, find the right docs, understand what “ready” means, and recover from confusion without guessing.

## Validation

- `go test ./...`
- `scripts/verify.sh --fast`
- `scripts/run-e2e.sh --no-go --no-tui --web-timeout 900`
- targeted `install.sh --status` validation for generated `FIRST_STEPS.txt` and `SERVICE_STATUS.txt`
- targeted `install.sh --doctor` validation for grouped summary output
