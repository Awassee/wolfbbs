# WolfBBS v1.1.11

## Summary
This release ships the first roadmap tranche after v1.1.10.

Delivered in this release:
- public community calendar at `/events`
- sysop event management at `/admin/events`
- caller-managed watched boards from `/boards`
- daily caller brief at `/today`
- updated help, quick-jump, and route primers for the new surfaces
- a documented 50-item product roadmap in `docs/ROADMAP_50.md`

## Why it matters
WolfBBS already had broad surface coverage, but the product still lacked a clear daily loop and a concrete scheduled-activity layer. This release closes that gap.

The result:
- callers can narrow boards to what they actually care about
- sysops can schedule visible reasons for callers to return
- callers have a single page that answers what matters today
